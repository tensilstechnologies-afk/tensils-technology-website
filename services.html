HOW WE WORK

One process, from first call to everyday support.

Every project — a five-page website or a full ERP — moves through the same three stages, so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

STEP 01

Kick-off

  • Discovery call to understand your business and goals
  • Scope, pages and features agreed in writing
  • Fixed quote and timeline before any work starts
  • Content and access checklist shared with you
STEP 02

Build

  • Design and build on a staging link you can follow live
  • Your feedback rounds folded in as we go
  • Tested on mobile, tablet and desktop before launch
  • Go-live handled by us — domain, hosting and checks
STEP 03

Support

  • Handover walkthrough so your team can run it daily
  • Updates, backups and small fixes after launch
  • One WhatsApp message away when something breaks
  • Room to grow — new features added as you scale
